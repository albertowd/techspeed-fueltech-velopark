# Changelog

## 0.8.0

### Both games

- Rounded metal roofs on the buildings in the middle of the track.
- Darker roofs on the paddock buildings.
- Race control tower windows with top lights opened out.
- See-through windows in steel frames on every building, with rooms behind them.
- Rooms lit at night by their ceiling lamps.
- Big white tent by the pit boxes.
- Fuel station sign turned to the road, with the Velopark logo and fuel prices, lit at night.
- Flagpoles at the 1500 start moved to where the real ones stand.

## 0.7.0

### Both games

- Renamed to Techspeed FuelTech Velopark: delete the old `techspeed_velopark` folder.
- The ground and the track follow the real ground's heights.
- New dirt roads along the north and west of the property.
- New fences round the property, the 1000 and the south pits.
- New buildings round the track: sheds, houses, a barn, the judges' tower, the Velogrill and a sports court.
- Paddock buildings repainted in grey block with salmon pillars.
- New race control tower, lit at night with Custom Shaders Patch.
- New fuel station by the pit exit.
- Techspeed banners on the stand and on the south fence.
- Flag marshal tents and flagpoles at the starts.
- Eucalyptus plantation along the north and west.
- More trees round the property: woods, broad leaf trees, palms and bamboo.
- You can hit the tree trunks.
- Tyre walls, poles and trees no longer float above the ground.
- Concrete paving by the 1500's top hairpin and on the run-offs.
- Yellow round kerbs through the middle of the track.
- Asphalt paths across the infield.
- Painted parking bays in the car park.
- New line painted across the road at the junction where the 1500 turns off.
- Distance boards can be knocked over.
- Start gantry legs as on the real gantry.
- No more bumps where two roads meet.
- No more invisible walls on the asphalt.
- Grass instead of asphalt round two of the 1000's corners.
- The pit lane asphalt no longer looks stretched.

### BeamNG.drive

- New download name: `techspeed-fueltech-velopark-0.7.0-beamng.zip`.
- AI race on every layout, against up to 35 karts.
- The time trials show in BeamNG's Time Trials list.
- Every layout's cones and boards in free roam.
- Pole lights on at night only.
- Softer grass, so tyres no longer sink into it.
- Infield paths and pit lanes on the map.
- Cones with a white band.
- Karts face the right way on the grid.
- Races no longer show 1969 as their date.

## 0.5.0

### Layouts

- Renamed to Techspeed Velopark, in the new folder `techspeed_velopark`: delete any old `velopark` folder.
- New 1500 Amateur: the rental layout with the easier inner corners.
- New 1500 Short: turns in on the S and skips the northern loop.
- New 1500 Short Amateur: the Short with the easier inner corners.
- New 1500 championship layout, with its pits at the centre building.
- The old 1500 is now 1500 Hotlap.
- New Kids layout.
- Hotlap mode starts just after the last sector line, so the first full lap is timed.

### Racing

- AI karts race on every layout instead of retiring to the pits.
- One clean racing line on every layout.
- More karts: 24 on the 1000, 16 on the 1500, 36 on the Hotlap, 32 on the 2500.
- Each pit box holds a team of two karts.
- Pit boxes painted on the ground on every layout.
- Grid spots moved behind their painted brackets.
- Hotlap and 2500 pit lane moved onto the pit apron, leaving by the real exit.
- Each layout shows only its own pit sign.
- Cones close the roads each layout does not use.

### Track

- New ground texture from recent satellite photos.
- Tyre walls rebuilt where the real ones stand, with tyre rings round the poles.
- Tyre walls along the pit entry, the pit lane and the central buildings.
- The ground between the tracks raised to its real height.
- More asphalt run-off on the 1000's corners.
- Wider pit asphalt, joined to the track with no gaps.
- No more gaps between the track and the grass.
- Kerbs no longer buried under asphalt or grass.
- Trees removed from the track side of the walls.

### Buildings

- New pit building based on the real one.
- New paddock buildings round the track.
- Centre building rebuilt, with a solar roof over the pits.
- White entry gate posts on the Kids layout.
- New start gantry with start lights on the 1000, 1500 and 2500.
- The gantry board shows the top 10 and the race time live (needs Custom Shaders Patch).
- The gantry lights show green, yellow under caution and red on a black flag (needs Custom Shaders Patch).

### Custom Shaders Patch

- 3D grass.
- Rain effects.
- Night lights on the poles.

### Interface

- Layout thumbnails show north up.
