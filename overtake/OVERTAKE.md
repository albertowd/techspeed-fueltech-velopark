[HEADING=1]Techspeed FuelTech Velopark[/HEADING]

The Techspeed FuelTech Velopark karting circuit in Nova Santa Rita, Brazil, built for go-karts. Eight layouts, from a 227 m kids track to a 2.5 km endurance layout, on ground traced from satellite imagery.

[LIST]
[*][B]Velopark 1000:[/B] rental and amateur track to learn go-kart racing, with more curves and less speed but still very challenging to new drivers.
[*][B]Velopark 1500:[/B] championship track with greater straight speed and tight turns, currently the most used track.
[*][B]Velopark 1500 Amateur:[/B] rental track with greater straight speed and not so tight turns, a new challenge for rental drivers.
[*][B]Velopark 1500 Short:[/B] short version of the 1500, used for quick races in championships.
[*][B]Velopark 1500 Short Amateur:[/B] short version of the Amateur, used for quick races in rental go-karts.
[*][B]Velopark 1500 Hotlap:[/B] used at night with real daily cars, for the public to get some circuit experience.
[*][B]Velopark 2500:[/B] a long race layout that combines the 1500 and the 1000, used in 2h, 4h, 6h and 12h races overnight.
[*][B]Velopark Kids:[/B] a kids track managed by a professor, teaching kids who are not old enough to race on the real track.
[/LIST]

[HEADING=2]Features[/HEADING]

[LIST]
[*]Start gantry on the 1000 and 1500 start lines, with start lights facing both ways.
[*]Live board on the gantry with the top 10, kart numbers, lap times and race time.
[*]Gantry lights stay green during the session, blink yellow under caution and turn red on a black flag.
[*]Pit building with open garages and a viewing hall, and a solar roof over the centre pits.
[*]Tyre walls placed on the real barrier lines, with pole tyre rings.
[*]Painted pit box brackets, with two karts per box.
[*]Each layout closes its unused roads with cones that can be knocked over.
[*]AI lines and pit lanes on every layout.
[*]3D grass, rain effects and night lights with Custom Shaders Patch.
[/LIST]

[HEADING=2]Requirements[/HEADING]

Custom Shaders Patch is recommended. It adds the grass, rain effects, night lights, the live board and the green, yellow and red gantry lights. The track still loads without it, and the start lights still work, but those features are missing.

[HEADING=2]Installing[/HEADING]

Copy the [B]techspeed_fueltech_velopark[/B] folder into [B]assettocorsa/content/tracks[/B]. If you have an older version in a [B]techspeed_velopark[/B], [B]velopark[/B] or [B]kart_velopark[/B] folder, delete that folder.

Servers need this same version of the track. The Kids layout must be added to the server as its own layout.
