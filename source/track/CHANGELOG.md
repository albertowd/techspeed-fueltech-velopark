# Changelog

## 0.7.0

Renamed to Techspeed FuelTech Velopark. The track now lies on the real ground's heights, with the buildings, fences and trees round it rebuilt, and BeamNG.drive gets AI races.

- Renamed to Techspeed FuelTech Velopark, in the new folder `techspeed_fueltech_velopark`: delete the old `techspeed_velopark` folder.

### Both games

- Ground and track at the real ground's heights; no grade over 2.7%.
- Dirt roads along the north and west of the property.
- Fences round the property, the vp_1000 and the south pits.
- Buildings round the track: sheds, houses, barn, judges' tower, Velogrill and sports court.
- Paddock buildings in grey block with salmon pillars.
- Race control tower, lit at night with Custom Shaders Patch.
- Fuel station in the R27 loop.
- Techspeed banners by R26 and on the south fence.
- Flag marshal tents and flagpoles at the starts.
- 3D eucalyptus plantation along the north and west.
- Woods, broad leaf trees, palms and bamboo round the property.
- Tree trunks can be hit.
- Tyre walls, poles and trees stand on the ground.
- Concrete paving on R29 and the floor kerb run-offs.
- Yellow round kerbs through the middle of the track.
- Asphalt paths across the infield.
- Car park asphalt with painted bays.
- Line across the road before T12.
- Distance boards can be knocked over.
- Start gantry legs as on the real gantry.
- No more steps between roads.
- No more invisible walls on the asphalt.
- Grass instead of asphalt round K1_T3 and K1_T4.
- Asphalt texture no longer stretched on the pit lanes.

### BeamNG.drive

- New download name: `techspeed-fueltech-velopark-0.7.0-beamng.zip`.
- AI race on every layout, against up to 35 karts.
- Time trials listed in BeamNG's Time Trials.
- Every layout's cones and boards in free roam.
- Pole lights on at night only.
- Softer grass, so tyres don't sink.
- Infield paths and pit lanes on the map.
- Cones with a white band.
- Karts face the right way on the grid.
- Races no longer show 1969 as their date.

## 0.6.0

The track for BeamNG.drive, with races and a working start gantry, and a few fixes.

### Assetto Corsa

- The light pole by the 1500 pit entry stands inside its ring of tyres, now closed all round,
  with its night light moved up with it.
- Grass grows outside the 1500's north turn too.
- The kids track's old rumble strips are replaced by the simple painted ramp kerbs, along the
  edge of the road outside its white line.

### BeamNG.drive

A separate download, `velopark-beamng-0.6.0.zip`: drop it in BeamNG's `mods` folder.

- The track of the 1500, with every layout's start to pick from in the level menu, each with
  its picture.
- A timed race for every layout but the hotlap: drive into the marker at the layout's start
  and press the key it shows. Pick the laps and a standing start on the grid or a rolling one
  from the hotlap start, then race through the countdown, with checkpoints, sector splits
  and lap times.
- The start gantry's board shows the ten best laps driven on the layout raced under it and the
  race clock, or the time of day, as in Assetto Corsa. Its start lamps light through the
  countdown and go out at the start.
- The night lights of the poles, lit by day too, as BeamNG has no lights that come on at night
  only.
- Grass near the racing line, where Assetto Corsa grows its own.
- Every layout's racing line on the map, which BeamNG's AI drives. No road traffic.
- Trees and painted numbers show through where they should, the tyre walls have open tops,
  and cars drive on the road, not over it.

## 0.5.1

Test build for the new kerbs, light poles and start panel.

- New kerbs on the Techspeed Velopark 1500 opening corners, modelled on the real ones:
  - Painted ramp on the inside of the first kink and of the Amateur corner after it.
  - Painted ramp with concrete ribs behind it on the inside of the left after the kink,
    of the hairpin round the grass island and of the far hairpin.
  - Flat kerb with grooves cut into it in place of the straight kerb on the outside of the exit
    from the hairpin round the grass island, moved out to where the real one is, in line with
    the grid.
  - All the other kerbs round the 1500 and its short layout: painted ramps, with concrete ribs
    on the second on the right up the third straight, inside the curve at the end of the
    long straight back to the middle and inside the top curve of the 1500, and flat kerbs with grooves along the two straights
    between the hairpins after the second S and outside the last corner.
  - New kerbs all round the Techspeed Velopark 1000 too: concrete ribs inside its corners,
    painted ramps elsewhere.
  - Narrow and low like the real ones: 0.6 m for the ramps, a 0.45 m ramp with 1 m of ribs behind.
- The white edge line of the road along every new kerb, where the old kerbs hid it, and along
  the grass edges of the roads between the layouts. On the 1000 it runs across the mouths of
  the roads the layout does not take, dashed at the pit entry and exit.
- The 1500 grid's lines, slots and start places follow the inside edge of the last turn where
  they end.
- The grass islands round the kerbs at the hairpins are grass all the way to the road, with an
  edge line along their flat side.
- Asphalt run-offs behind some of the 1000's kerbs, with the tyre barrier moved out along them.
- Grid slot marks on the second straight, the lines along its outside edge and the pit lane
  beside it, and the hotlap start line after the turn onto the third straight, as painted
  on the real track.
- A 50 m board on the second straight, after its 100 m board.
- New tyre walls: on the island along the exit onto the second straight, where the tarmac ends
  inside the turn onto the third straight, and across the grass to the barrier at the exit of
  the kink after the second S. The gaps in the barrier round the inside of the 1500 pit entry
  and of the 1000's second corner are closed.
- New 17 m light poles with a ring of round floodlights and a stack of tyres round the foot,
  in place of the old 8 m poles.
- Night lights moved up to the new lamp heads, at a quarter of the brightness that matched
  the old poles.
- The paddock buildings no longer glow brighter than anything else at night.
- Start panel lowered to the height of the real one, with its legs standing at the panel ends.
- The 1500 and 1000 start panels stand where the real ones do: the 1500's on its shadow in the
  ground photo, just before its start line, and the 1000's past its line, over the pit exit.
  On every layout the panel it does not use stands dark.
- Removed copies of some trees that were left far under the ground.
- A first test conversion of the 1500 for BeamNG.drive, as a separate download.

## 0.5.0

Everything new since 0.3.1.

### Layouts

- Track renamed to Techspeed Velopark, in the new folder `techspeed_velopark`, to avoid conflicts with the other Velopark mod. If you have an older `velopark` folder, delete it.
- New Techspeed Velopark 1500 Amateur: rental layout that uses the easier inner corners.
- New Techspeed Velopark 1500 Short: turns in on the S and skips the northern loop.
- New Techspeed Velopark 1500 Short Amateur: Short with the easier inner corners.
- New Techspeed Velopark 1500 championship layout, with its pits at the centre building.
- The old Techspeed Velopark 1500 is now Techspeed Velopark 1500 Hotlap.
- New Techspeed Velopark Kids layout.
- Hotlap mode starts just after the last sector line, so the first full lap is timed.

### Racing

- AI karts now race on every layout instead of going to the pits and retiring.
- Racing line display fixed: one clean line of arrows on every layout.
- More karts: 24 on Techspeed Velopark 1000, 16 on Techspeed Velopark 1500, 36 on Hotlap, 32 on Techspeed Velopark 2500.
- Each pit box holds a team of two karts.
- Pit box brackets are painted on the ground on every layout.
- Grid spots moved back behind their painted brackets.
- Hotlap and 2500 pit lane moved onto the pit apron, leaving by the real exit.
- Each layout shows only its own PIT card at its own pit entry.
- Cones close the roads each layout does not use.

### Track

- New ground texture from recent satellite imagery.
- Tyre walls rebuilt along the real marks on the ground, and pole tyre rings added.
- Tyre walls along the pit entry, the pit lane and the central buildings.
- The ground between the tracks is raised to its real height, with smooth ramps at the entries.
- Extra asphalt run-off on the Techspeed Velopark 1000 corners.
- Pit asphalt widened and joined to the track with no grass gaps.
- Gaps between the track and the grass closed all around.
- Kerbs are no longer buried under asphalt or grass.
- Trees removed from the track side of the walls.

### Buildings

- New pit building based on the real one: closed garages on one wing, an open viewing hall on the other.
- New paddock buildings around the track.
- Centre building rebuilt: solar roof over the pits, with a drive-through garage in the middle.
- White entry gate posts on the Kids layout.
- New start gantry with start lights on both sides, on the Techspeed Velopark 1000, 1500 and 2500 start lines.
- The gantry board shows the top 10, kart numbers, lap times and race time live (needs Custom Shaders Patch).
- The gantry lights stay green during the session, blink yellow under caution and turn red on a black flag (needs Custom Shaders Patch).

### Effects (Custom Shaders Patch)

- 3D grass on the ground.
- Rain effects.
- Night lights on the poles.

### Interface

- Layout thumbnails now show north up.
