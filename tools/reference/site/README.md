# The real site

Frames from on-board and walking videos at the track, to model from. North to south through the
middle of the track:

- The storage building (the garage on the objects map): kart parts and the helpers' karts.
- The shed beside it, with spare rental karts; the white tent on the map is not in these frames,
  only its steel arches over the parked karts, and stays.
- The pit building: a big kart workshop, karts and parts inside to repair them, doors on both
  sides of the pits (15-17).
- The race control building (05, 06, 13, 14).
- The bar, with tables, chairs and people (06, 07, 13).
- Two raised wooden stands for spectators east of the 1500's straight: a small one on posts among
  the trees (08), and south of it the one with the Techspeed banner (08, 09).
- On the south straight, the industry south of the circuit, the biggest building there (09, 10).
- The fuel station (11, 12).

01-03: the road out of the pits by the shed: light concrete where the karts park, a yellow line
where it meets the asphalt, a curved concrete kerb painted yellow with tyres and orange poles,
and a narrow path on to the track. 13-17: the pit entrance and the pits.

18-27: the vp_1000's pit entry and lane, in the rain, along the east side of the same middle
buildings: the lane runs south beside the storage building (a yellow line, tyres along its wall,
louvred doors, a fire hose box, a pressure washer) and climbs a long ramp, a concrete retaining
wall on its right with the shed's karts parked on the raised ground behind it, up to the pit
building, so the pits stand at one height for both tracks. Then the pit boxes under the roof on
that side (25) and the workshop inside the pit building (26, 27): steel roof trusses, fluorescent
lights, grey walls, a tool cabinet, benches and karts under repair.
