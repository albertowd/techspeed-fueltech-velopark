    Road editor

    Wheelzoom at the cursor
    Drag on empty map, right or middle drag, Space + dragpan
    Click a roadselect it (its nodes, handles and widths show)
    Drag a node (square)move it, its handles with it
    Drag a handle (circle)turn and stretch the curve; the other handle follows (a smooth node). Alt + drag: a corner
    Drag a width diamond (L, R)that side's width at the node; Shift: both sides
    Click a line or rumbleselect it (on any road)
    Drag a line or rumbleslide it along its road, as long as it is
    Drag a line's or rumble's end (triangle)where it starts or ends along the road
    A then click on the roada node there (the curve kept); click past an end: the road goes on to there
    N then click, click…, Entera new road through those points
    J on a road's first or last nodejoin it to the nearest other road's end (within 20 m): one point, straight on, as wide, no gap between them. Moving either moves both; Unjoin in the node's form
    Deletethe selected node (or line, or rumble); Shift+Delete or Delete road: the selected road
    B or Bring ina road the track has but the editor not yet, from its mesh
    Ssmooth the selected node's handles (or every node's, with none selected)
    Dragging a nodeit snaps to another road's end, centre line or edges within 10 pixels (a cyan ring says to what); Ctrl: no snapping
    M then dragmeasure between two points (snapping too)
    Ccheck the surface where you look (up to 150 m across): built as the build makes it, every region merged; its seams drawn, any problem ringed red
    Escback to Select, nothing selected
    Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Sundo / redo / save

    Left and right are as seen going along the road from its first node. s is how far along the road (metres): lines and rumbles are placed by it. A road changed in shape gets its heights again at the next build.
